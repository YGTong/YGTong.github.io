Hello!

I am a Ph.D. at the Intelligent Medical Equipment Diagnosis‑Treatment (IMED) Research Center, Xi'an Jiaotong University, under the supervision of Prof. Jianhua Ma and Assoc. Prof. Shipeng Wang.

My research sits at the intersection of medical AI, intelligent imaging, and embodied intelligence. My main research interests include CT scatter correction, medical Agent, surgical navigation robots, uncertainty‑aware learning, and robotic systems bridging visual understanding and physical action.

I am always happy to discuss research ideas, open-source projects, and potential collaborations.

Research interests

Medical imaging

CT X-ray scatter correction, physics-informed neural networks, and learning-based methods for reliable diagnosis.

Multimodal perception

Thermal-RGB sensing, semantic segmentation, uncertainty quantification, and non-destructive inspection.

Embodied intelligence

Medical embodied intelligence, interventional and puncture surgical navigation robots, and perception‑action systems for complex environments.

Agent

LLM 4 Medical, Vlm 4 Medical, Foundation Model, Agent 4 Medical
